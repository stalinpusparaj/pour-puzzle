Pour Puzzle Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

Pour Puzzle ("the app") is a puzzle game for Android and iOS made by Stalin Pusparaj ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

In short: I don't ask for your name, email or any account, and I don't run servers that receive data about you. Your game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob and sells an optional "Remove Ads" purchase through RevenueCat; those services process some device data, as described below.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, daily-challenge streak) and settings (sound, language, colour-blind symbols, reduce motion) are saved only on your device. They are not sent to me. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices.

You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings. Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchase (RevenueCat)

If you buy "Remove Ads", payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; I never see your payment details. RevenueCat verifies and remembers the purchase so it can be restored. To do this it processes an anonymous app user ID, the purchase receipt and basic device information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Sharing

When you choose to share a result, the app creates the share card on your device and hands it to your phone's share menu. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

Analytics

The app does not currently send analytics to any service. If that changes, this policy will be updated before it does.

Children

Pour Puzzle is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

  • Change ad consent in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Buy "Remove Ads" to stop ads altogether.
  • Delete all locally stored game data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.
  • For requests about data held by Google or RevenueCat, use the links above or contact me and I will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
